Web3 Hackathon 2026
Welcome to the Web3 Hackathon 2026 by HackIndia, a nationwide innovation challenge focused on building real-world decentralized applications. This hackathon brings together developers, builders, and innovators to solve industry-backed problems using blockchain technology.

🧠 About the Hackathon
The Web3 Hackathon 2026 is designed to accelerate blockchain adoption by solving practical challenges in token ecosystems and on-chain identity.

Participants will work on problem statements provided by Sharp Economy, building scalable solutions that can be integrated into real-world applications.

This is your chance to build impactful Web3 products, gain recognition, and compete with top talent across the country.

🧩 Problem Statements (Powered by Sharp Economy)
🔹 Problem Statement 1: Plug-and-Play Sharp Token Integration Platform

Build a plug-and-play platform that allows any developer or company to seamlessly integrate Sharp Token into their application or website.

Requirements:

Easy-to-use SDK, APIs, or no-code integration

Support core functionalities:

Earn Sharp Tokens

Spend Sharp Tokens

Buy Sharp Tokens

Developer-friendly documentation

Scalable and secure infrastructure

Objective:
Enable frictionless adoption of Sharp Token across multiple platforms.

🔹 Problem Statement 2: Skills On-Chain Platform

Create a skills-on-chain platform that enables issuing and verifying credentials on the blockchain.

Requirements:

Issue certifications/credentials as on-chain records

Store and verify credentials via Polygon blockchain*

Allow third-party apps/websites to integrate this system

Provide instant and trustless verification

Objective:

Build a decentralized system where skills and credentials are verifiable, tamper-proof, and portable.
